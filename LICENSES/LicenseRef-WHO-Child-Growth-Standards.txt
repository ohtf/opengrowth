WHO Child Growth Standards data

The WHO Child Growth Standards (WHO Multicentre Growth Reference Study
Group, 2006) are published by the World Health Organization, which holds
copyright in them.

These files are copied unmodified from the rcpchgrowth Python package
(version 4.6.5, published on PyPI by the Royal College of Paediatrics and
Child Health), which transcribed them from the WHO's published tables.

WHO publishes these tables for public use. The specific terms that apply to
their redistribution in software have not yet been recorded by opengrowth
(roadmap item R2). The opengrowth software licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later)
does not apply to these files and grants no rights in them.
