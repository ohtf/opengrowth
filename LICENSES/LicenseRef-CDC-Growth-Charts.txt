CDC 2000 growth charts data

The CDC 2000 growth charts (Kuczmarski RJ, Ogden CL, Guo SS, et al. 2000
CDC Growth Charts for the United States: methods and development. Vital
Health Stat 11. 2002;(246):1-190) were produced by the National Center for
Health Statistics of the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. As
a work of the United States federal government, they are not subject to
copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. 105).

These files are copied unmodified from the rcpchgrowth Python package
(version 4.6.5, published on PyPI by the Royal College of Paediatrics and
Child Health), which transcribed them, including the extended BMI-for-age
sigma values, from the CDC's published tables.

The terms that apply to their redistribution in software outside the
United States have not yet been recorded by opengrowth (roadmap item R2).
The opengrowth software licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later) does not apply to these
files and grants no rights in them.
