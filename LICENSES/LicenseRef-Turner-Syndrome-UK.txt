UK Turner syndrome growth reference (1985)

LMS values for the height of girls with Turner syndrome, from Lyon AJ,
Preece MA, Grant DB. Growth curve for girls with Turner syndrome. Archives
of Disease in Childhood 1985;60:932-935. The owner of these values has not
yet been recorded.

This file is copied unmodified from the rcpchgrowth Python package
(version 4.6.5, published on PyPI by the Royal College of Paediatrics and
Child Health), where it is publicly distributed.

opengrowth embeds it under the maintainer decision of 2026-11-18 that every
reference table is embedded unless its owner requires its removal
(spec/standard.md section 3.3). The terms that apply to its redistribution
have not yet been recorded (roadmap item R2). The opengrowth software
