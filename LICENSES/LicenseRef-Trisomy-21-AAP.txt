US Down syndrome (Trisomy 21) growth charts (2015)

LMS values from Zemel BS, Pipan M, Stallings VA, et al. Growth charts for
children with Down syndrome in the United States. Pediatrics
2015;136(5):e1204-e1211, published by the American Academy of Pediatrics.
The owner of these values has not yet been recorded.

These files are copied unmodified from the rcpchgrowth Python package
(version 4.6.5, published on PyPI by the Royal College of Paediatrics and
Child Health), where they are publicly distributed.

opengrowth embeds them under the maintainer decision of 2026-11-18 that every
reference table is embedded unless its owner requires its removal
(spec/standard.md section 3.3). The terms that apply to their redistribution
have not yet been recorded (roadmap item R2). The opengrowth software
licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later) does not apply to these files and grants no
rights in them.
