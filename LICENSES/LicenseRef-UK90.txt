UK90 growth reference data

The UK90 growth reference (Cole TJ, Freeman JV, Preece MA, 1998, and
related publications) was produced under the Medical Research Council. The
Medical Research Council's functions passed to UK Research and Innovation
(UKRI) in 2018, and UKRI now owns the data.

No licence for redistribution or for production use has been granted to
opengrowth or to the Open Health Technology Foundation. A licence
application to UKRI is pending.

These files are copied unmodified from the rcpchgrowth Python package
(version 4.6.5, published on PyPI by the Royal College of Paediatrics and
Child Health), where they are already publicly distributed. They are
included in opengrowth for pre-alpha research and development only, by
decision of the project maintainer recorded in spec/queries.md (Q1).

They must not be used to calculate values for the care of any patient until
a licence has been obtained. The opengrowth software licence
(AGPL-3.0-or-later) does not apply to these files and grants no rights in
them.
