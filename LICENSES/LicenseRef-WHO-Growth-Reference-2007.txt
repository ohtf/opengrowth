WHO Growth Reference data for 5 to 19 years (2007)

The WHO Growth Reference for school-aged children and adolescents (de Onis
M, Onyango AW, Borghi E, Siyam A, Nishida C, Siekmann J. Development of a
WHO growth reference for school-aged children and adolescents. Bulletin of
the World Health Organization 2007;85:660-667) is published by the World
Health Organization, which holds copyright in it.

This file is copied unmodified from the rcpchgrowth Python package (version
4.6.5, published on PyPI by the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child
Health), which transcribed it from the WHO's published tables.

WHO publishes these tables for public use. The specific terms that apply to
their redistribution in software have not yet been recorded by opengrowth
(roadmap item R2). The opengrowth software licence (AGPL-3.0-or-later) does
not apply to this file and grants no rights in it.
