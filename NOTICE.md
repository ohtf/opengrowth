## Methods

- The LMS method: Cole TJ, Green PJ. Smoothing reference centile curves: the LMS method and penalized likelihood. *Statistics in Medicine* 1992;11:1305-1319.
- Four-point Lagrange cubic interpolation of LMS values, as used in Tim Cole's LMSgrowth. Reimplemented from the published method; no LMSgrowth source is included.
- The normal distribution and its inverse: SciPy's `ndtr` and `ndtri`, from the Cephes Math Library by Stephen L. Moshier (see Third-party code).
