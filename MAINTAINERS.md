## Roles we are looking for

- **A second maintainer**, so that no release, review, or security fix depends on one person.
- **An independent clinical reviewer**: a paediatrician or growth specialist who was not involved in the port, to review clinically significant changes and the UK-WHO port (`R35`). At present the author and the Clinical Safety Officer are the same person, which is a weakness the safety case should not have to carry.

If you can help with either, open an issue or contact the OHTF through [openhealthtechnology.org](https://openhealthtechnology.org).

## How maintainers are added

Following the OHTF [do-ocracy](https://openhealthtechnology.org/governance/do-ocracy/): people who contribute sustained, careful work are invited by the existing maintainers. A maintainer who steps back is listed as emeritus rather than removed.
